Terms & Privacy

Tymora is a free planning app built and run by a school student in Kolkata, India ("I", "me"). This page explains the rules for using it and what I do with your information, in plain language.

Terms of Use  ·  Privacy Policy

Terms of Use

1. What Tymora is

Tymora helps you plan study time and tasks. It is a personal project, offered free and "as is". It may change, break or go offline, and I can't promise it will always be available or that every schedule it makes is right. You are responsible for your own deadlines, so please don't rely on Tymora for anything where a mistake would be serious.

The syllabus chapters in Tymora were typed in by me and may contain mistakes or go out of date. Tymora is not affiliated with or endorsed by CISCE, the ISC board, or any school.

2. Your account

  • You choose a username and password. Keep your password to yourself, and you are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • You don't need to use your real name. A nickname is fine.
  • One person per account, please.
  • If you are under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian knows you are using Tymora and is okay with it.

3. How you may use it

Please don't try to break, overload or hack Tymora, don't use anyone else's account, and don't use it for anything illegal or harmful. I can suspend or delete accounts that do this.

4. Your content

The tasks, commitments and other things you enter are yours. By using Tymora you allow me to store and process them for one purpose only: running Tymora for you.

5. Data loss and availability

Tymora runs on free hosting services, which can pause, change or fail, and the database does not have automatic backups. Your data could be lost or unavailable without warning. Don't treat Tymora as the only place something important is written down.

6. No guarantees, limited responsibility

Tymora is provided without warranties of any kind. To the extent the law allows, I am not responsible for losses caused by using it or being unable to use it, including missed deadlines or lost data.

7. Ending things

You can stop using Tymora whenever you like and ask me to delete your account (see the Privacy section). I may change, pause or stop Tymora at any time.

8. Changes and law

I may update these terms and will change the date at the top when I do. Continuing to use Tymora after a change means you accept it. These terms are governed by the laws of India.

Privacy Policy

1. What I collect

  • Account details: your username, and your password, which is stored only as a one-way scrambled hash. I can't see or recover your actual password.
  • Profile: whether you are an ISC student, your class, and the subjects you chose.
  • What you enter: tasks (subject, chapter, deadline, difficulty, time estimate, number of sessions), recurring and one-off commitments with their times and colours, and your sleep hours.
  • Progress: which tasks you've completed, your XP, levels and streaks.

I do not collect your email, phone number, location or contacts, and Tymora has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. If that ever changes, I will update this page first.

2. Cookies

Tymora uses one cookie, a session cookie that keeps you logged in. It is needed for the app to work. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

3. Services that handle your data

  • Vercel runs the app.
  • Supabase hosts the database where your data is stored. I chose the Mumbai (India) region.
  • When you load a page, your browser fetches fonts and scripts from Google Fonts, jsDelivr and the Tailwind CDN. As with any website, those providers can see your IP address and browser details.

4. How I use your data

Only to run Tymora for you: to build your schedule, show your progress and keep your account working. I don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else. As the person who runs the app, I can technically see what is in the database. I only look when I need to fix a problem or help you, and I won't browse your tasks out of curiosity or share them.

5. How long I keep it

Until you ask me to delete your account. When I delete an account, your profile, tasks, commitments and progress are removed from the database.

6. Your choices

You can ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and everything in it. Contact me at {{ contact }} and I'll do it as soon as I can. If you forget your password, contact me the same way and I'll reset it.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed and the site runs over HTTPS. I take care, but no online service is perfectly secure, so use a password you don't use anywhere else.

8. Young users

Tymora is built for school students. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian's knowledge. A parent or guardian can contact me at the address above to ask about or delete a child's account.

9. Changes

If I change how I handle data, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
