How Yetra works

Tell Yetra what already fills your week and what you need to get done. It finds the gaps and places your work in them, and re-plans when anything changes.

Quick start (about 5 minutes)

  1. 1

    Set your sleep hours

    On the Commitments page. Yetra never schedules over sleep, and hides it from your calendar.

  2. 2

    Add what's already taking your time

    School, tuition, sports, meals: anything that repeats weekly goes under Recurring. One-time things (a dentist visit, a family dinner) go under One-off. Pick a colour for each so your calendar is easy to read.

  3. 3

    Add your first task

    Choose the subject and chapter, a deadline, how hard it is and how long you think it will take. Yetra places it in your free time right away.

  4. 4

    Check the calendar, do the work, tick it off

    Mark a task done from the dashboard to earn XP and keep your streak going.

How scheduling works

Yetra splits every day into 30-minute slots. A slot is free if nothing else is using it: not sleep, not a recurring commitment, not a one-off event, not another task.

  • Deadline within 7 days: Yetra re-plans these every time something changes (a new task, a new commitment, a task completed). The most urgent go first, and when deadlines tie, shorter tasks go first.
  • Deadline further away, or none: placed once in the next free time after that 7-day window, then left alone so your future doesn't keep shuffling.
  • Harder tasks get extra time: about 10% more per difficulty level, because hard things run long.
  • Each session needs one unbroken block. A 90-minute task needs 90 free minutes in a row.
  • Can't fit it? Yetra won't squeeze it in badly. It leaves it unscheduled and tells you which task, so you can fix it.

Tasks

  • Subject and chapter: ISC students pick from their own subjects, and the chapter list matches their class. Not listed? Use "Not in the list? Type your own". Everyone else types their own subject and chapter.
  • Difficulty (1 to 5): how hard the work is for the content, not for you. It sets the extra time and the XP.
  • Time: your honest estimate in minutes. Aim for realistic, not optimistic.
  • Sessions: leave at 1 for a single sitting. A 6-hour video won't fit in one gap, so split it into 3 or 4 sessions and Yetra spreads them across days.
  • Live preview: on the task form, the panel shows exactly how much time Yetra will reserve.

Calendar

  • Yellow blocks are your scheduled tasks. Tap one for details and an Edit button.
  • Your recurring and one-off commitments appear in the colours you picked.
  • Use Prev and Next to move between weeks. On a phone you'll see one day at a time, with tabs along the top.
  • Deleting a commitment removes the whole block, and Yetra re-plans your tasks around the new free time.

XP, levels and streaks

Streaks work for everyone. Complete at least one task in a day to keep your streak going.

  • Miss a day and your streak resets, unless you have a streak shield, which covers one missed day.
  • You earn a shield for every 7-day streak and every time a subject levels up. You can hold up to 3.

XP and levels are for ISC students, since they're tied to your subjects.

  • Finishing a task earns XP based on how long and how hard it was. Finishing at least a day before the deadline gives a 20% bonus, and finishing late costs 20%.
  • Each subject has its own level and title (Novice, Apprentice, Adept, Expert, Master). Your overall journey level has its own titles (Traveller, Wayfarer, Pathfinder, Trailblazer, Voyager).
  • Level 2 needs 50 XP in a subject, level 3 needs 200, level 4 needs 450, and so on.
  • Un-ticking a completed task takes its XP back, so there's no farming.

Troubleshooting

"Could not schedule: [task]"

There isn't enough free time before the deadline. Try fewer minutes, more sessions, a later deadline, or removing a commitment you don't need that week.

My calendar looks empty

Check the week you're viewing, and make sure your task has a deadline or is new enough to have been scheduled. Tasks with no deadline are placed after the first 7 days.
